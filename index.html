About me

I'm a Biomedical Engineer who worked in a pharmaceutical company. At Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine, as Packaging Project Engineer, I led end-to-end projects in the production area, coordinating cross-departmental teams and multiple suppliers to meet timelines and budgets while ensuring GMP compliance.

One thing I've noticed across every project: results follow from engaging people and building a genuinely collaborative team. My years volunteering as an ambulance rescuer taught me the value of listening and a deep appreciation for empathy and teamwork — and my Business English studies in Dublin sharpened my ability to operate in international environments, where diverse perspectives generate the best ideas.

I'm naturally curious — I like to question and analyze what lies behind things, at work and in everyday life. My passion for art has trained me to notice small details, and scuba diving has taught me exactly how much they matter: a diver has to return to the surface at a precise speed of 9 m/min. That same curiosity is what's now pulling me toward an International MBA in Paris, to build the finance, marketing and management skills I want to bring back into engineering.
